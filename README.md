# cat-bond-pricing-analysis
# Mortality Analysis and Catastrophe Bond Pricing

## Overview

Academic group project developed as part of the Life Insurance Models course during my Erasmus+ year at Wrocław University of Science and Technology, within the MSc programme in Applied Mathematics.

The project explores mortality data and catastrophe bond pricing from a statistical and actuarial perspective.

## Methodology

- Exploratory analysis of mortality time series
- Analysis of logarithmic returns
- Autocorrelation analysis (ACF)
- Statistical distribution visualization
- Catastrophe bond pricing sensitivity analysis
- Interactive three-dimensional visualization of bond prices

## Objective

To explore mortality dynamics and understand how changes in pricing parameters, such as alpha and the trigger threshold, affect catastrophe bond prices.

## Technologies

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Statsmodels, Plotly and Jupyter Notebook.
